Four case studies, bank format

Full reports with their Excel models, written the way a bank or investment team would present them. Companies and clients are fictional; market data are real and cited.

01 — Credit analysis

Bank credit memo

USD 2.4m equipment term loan and USD 0.9m working-capital revolver for an Aleppo food manufacturer: 25 ratios, five-year CFADS, stress tests, collateral haircuts and a 12-factor rating. Recommendation: approve with conditions.

  • Min. DSCR 1.77x
  • LTV 39.9%
  • Rating BAC-4

02 — Project finance

Aleppo solar farm feasibility study

60 MWp plant selling power to factories in Sheikh Najjar Industrial City: capex, 60/40 debt and equity, PPA pricing, sensitivity tornado, 10,000-run Monte Carlo and a risk matrix.

  • NPV USD 9.9m
  • IRR 20.4%
  • LCOE USD 0.0785/kWh
  • Min. DSCR 1.91x

USD 500k portfolio proposal

Moderate-risk, ten-year mandate for a Gulf-based client: investment policy statement, ten real ETFs, constrained optimization, 2016–2026 backtest, VaR/CVaR and a Sharia-compliant variant.

  • CAGR 7.54%
  • Volatility 8.28%
  • Max drawdown −17.9%
